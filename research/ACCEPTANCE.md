## Release promotion rule

Synthetic controls validate instruments/functions; they do not demonstrate installed drivers, reference fidelity, clean installation or production reliability. Preserve failed originals and distinguish unavailable cases from passes.

Before promotion, complete strict independent sustained routing, real application/permission and hardware recovery journeys, then current signed/notarized distribution acceptance.
